FAQ

Questions, answered.

Need help with an existing order? Email support@hybridgrading.com

What is Glass-Box Grading™?

It's HGA's new grading standard. Instead of sending your cards into a "black box," you can follow your card from delivery to grade reveal — with card tracking, a guaranteed deadline, a full grading report with subgrades, and visible proof behind your grade.

When does the 15-business-day clock start?

Your clock starts the moment the carrier delivers your card to HGA. You'll see your final grade, subgrades, and grading report through your U-Collect portal within 15 business days, guaranteed.

What happens if HGA misses the deadline?

If your grade isn't revealed on time, we'll give you $1 in credit for every day we're late.

What's the difference between the Poly and Glass slots?

Poly ($29 pre-launch) gets your card professionally graded, protected, and verified. Glass ($200 pre-launch) includes everything in Poly, plus premium Glass + Metal housing, a museum-worthy card presentation, and a premium display experience for your most prized collectibles.

Is there an upcharge for high-value cards?

There's no card-value upcharge on cards valued under $10,000. For cards valued above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies.

How does RFID verification work?

Tap your card's label with your phone to access its permanent digital record. Each card is also blockchain-certified, adding a layer of protection against card swapping and fraudulent verification.

What card sizes can HGA grade?

HGA supports 60+ card sizes — from standard trading cards to oversized collectibles and booklet formats. If we don't have a size that fits your card, we'll create one that does.

Can I transfer my pre-launch purchase to someone else?

No. Pre-launch purchases are non-transferable and cannot be transferred to another person or account.

How does HGA work with U-Collect?

HGA handles the grading. U-Collect powers the connected experience around it — helping you track, verify, collect, trade and manage your cards in one ecosystem.

What card categories can I submit?

From sports and TCGs to entertainment and pop culture — including sports, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, UFC and Disney cards.
